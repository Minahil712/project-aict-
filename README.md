# project-aict-
This repository features a light bulb model developed in COMSOL Multiphysics that demonstrates the conversion of electrical energy into heat and light through coupled electrical and thermal physics. 
The model includes key components such as the filament, glass envelope, surrounding gas, and electrical terminals, with realistic geometry and material properties. Using the Electric Currents and Heat Transfer modules, it simulates Joule heating, temperature distribution, current density, and heat dissipation mechanisms. The results provide insight into energy efficiency, thermal behavior, and material performance, making the project a useful educational resource for students and researchers in physics, electrical engineering, and applied mathematics learning multiphysics simulation techniques.
